# Shed Design and Materials List

## Lumber
- **Pressure-treated 4x4 posts** (for foundation/support): 6 @ 8 feet  
- **2x4 lumber** (for framing walls and roof): 20 @ 8 feet  
- **2x6 lumber** (for floor joists and roof rafters): 10 @ 10 feet  
- **Plywood or OSB sheets** (for floor, walls, and roof sheathing): 12 @ 4'x8' sheets, 3/4" thickness  

## Fasteners
- **Galvanized nails**:  
  - 8d nails: 5 lbs (for framing)  
  - 16d nails: 5 lbs (for flooring and sheathing)  
- **Deck screws (3" and 1 5/8")**:  
  - 3" exterior wood screws: 1 lb (for structural framing connections)  
  - 1 5/8" exterior wood screws: 1 lb (for attaching sheathing)  
- **Joist hangers**: 10 (for securing floor joists)  
- **Hurricane ties or metal straps**: 10 (for added roof/frame reinforcement)  
- **Door hinges**: 2 heavy-duty hinges  
- **Door latch/lockset**: 1 set  

## Roofing
- **Asphalt shingles**: 3 bundles (enough to cover roof area with overlap)  
- **Roofing felt (tar paper)**: 1 roll (typically 15 lbs weight)  
- **Roofing nails**: 1 lb (for shingles)  
- **Drip edge flashing**: 40 linear feet (to protect edges of roof sheathing)  
- **Ridge vent** (optional): 1 piece matching roof length  

## Siding
- **Exterior siding panels** (T1-11 plywood or vinyl siding): 6 @ 4'x8' sheets  
- **Housewrap (weather-resistant barrier)**: 1 roll (to cover walls beneath siding)  

## Doors
- **Pre-hung exterior door**: 1 (36"x80" standard size for easy access)  
- OR  
- **Materials to build a door**:  
  - 2x4 lumber: 3 @ 8 feet (if building door frame)  
  - 1/2" plywood panel: 1 sheet 4'x8'  
  - Door hinges and latch (included above)  

## Tools
- **Measuring tape**  
- **Carpenter's square**  
- **Circular saw or handsaw**  
- **Hammer**  
- **Cordless drill with screwdriver bits**  
- **Level**  
- **Ladder (6-8 feet)**  
- **Safety gear** (gloves, eye protection, dust mask)  
- **Chalk line**  
- **Utility knife**  
- **Tin snips** (for metal flashing)  
- **Caulking gun** (for sealing around windows, doors, and trim)  

---

This list assumes a basic rectangular shed approximately 8'x10' with a pitched roof. Adjust quantities and sizes depending on your specific shed dimensions and design preferences.
