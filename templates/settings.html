General

Preferences

These settings are saved to your signed-in Nova account.

Chat

Enter sends

Turn this off to use Enter for a new line. Shift+Enter continues to insert a line break.

New conversations are automatically named from a meaningful first message. Greetings are skipped, and manual titles are kept.

Appearance

Theme

Choose Nova's dark, light, or device-matched colors.

Voice

Voice uses your browser's speech recognition when available. Speak reads the latest reply aloud with browser text-to-speech. Voice choice depends on your device, so Nova does not offer a separate unsupported voice selector.

Projects

Project actions and approvals are controlled in the project workspace. Settings do not change execution safety or approval requirements.

Memory & personalization

Nova Memory stores information you choose to save for future conversations. Manage saved items in the Memory panel in Nova.

Privacy & data

Manage individual conversations from their row menu. Project deletion is available in Projects and asks for confirmation.

Account, security, and billing
