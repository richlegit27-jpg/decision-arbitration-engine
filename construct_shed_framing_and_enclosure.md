# Construct Shed Framing and Enclosure

## Install Roof and Exterior Shell

This step involves adding the roof framing, installing roofing materials, applying siding to the walls, and framing door openings to create a weather-resistant enclosure for your shed.

---

### 1. Roof Framing

- **Install Roof Rafters or Trusses**  
  - Measure and mark the layout for rafters or trusses on the top wall plates.  
  - Space rafters evenly, typically 16” or 24” on center depending on local codes and roof load requirements.  
  - Secure rafters to the top plates with appropriate metal connectors or framing nails.  
  - Add collar ties or ridge boards if applicable for added stability.

- **Install Roof Sheathing**  
  - Cover rafters with exterior grade plywood or OSB sheathing (typically ½” or ⅝” thickness).  
  - Stagger joints between sheets for stronger shear strength.  
  - Secure sheathing with nails or screws spaced per local building code.

### 2. Roofing Material

- **Apply Roofing Underlayment**  
  - Roll out roofing felt or synthetic underlayment over the roof sheathing.  
  - Overlap edges according to manufacturer instructions to create a waterproof barrier.  
  - Secure underlayment with staples or approved fasteners.

- **Install Roofing Material**  
  - Choose roofing material such as asphalt shingles, metal roofing panels, or other materials.  
  - Follow manufacturer instructions for installation.  
  - Start at the bottom edge of the roof and work upward, overlapping as you go.  
  - Install drip edge flashing around perimeter to direct water off the roof.

### 3. Siding Installation

- **Prepare Wall Surface**  
  - Wrap exterior walls with a weather-resistant barrier (house wrap or felt paper).  
  - Seal seams with appropriate tape to prevent water intrusion.

- **Install Siding Material**  
  - Common siding materials include wood, vinyl, metal, or composite panels.  
  - Start installation at the bottom of the walls, working upward.  
  - Use proper fasteners and leave space for expansion if needed.

### 4. Door Openings

- **Frame Door Openings**  
  - Identify location for door openings based on shed design.  
  - Frame rough openings with studs, headers, and trimmers sized to support the load above.  
  - Install sill plates and ensure openings are square and level.

- **Weatherproof Door Openings**  
  - Apply flashing tape around the perimeter of the openings.  
  - Install door jambs or frames according to door manufacturer specifications.

---

### Notes and Tips

- Verify all measurements and materials with local building codes and weather conditions.  
- Use treated lumber for sill plates and any framing in direct contact with the ground.  
- Consider ventilation options such as soffit vents or ridge vents to prevent moisture buildup in the roof.  
- Safety first: always use proper personal protective equipment when cutting and lifting materials.

---

Complete these steps to enclose the shed, creating a durable shell that protects the interior and framing from weather exposure.
