Nova Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Nova is an AI workspace assistant designed to help users think, plan, create, analyze, and solve problems.

Information We Collect

Nova may collect information you provide when using the service, including account information, messages, uploaded files, and preferences used to provide and improve the service.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate Nova, maintain conversations, provide requested features, improve reliability, and protect the service.

AI Processing

Messages and uploaded content may be processed by AI services to generate responses and provide requested functionality.

Data Storage

Nova stores certain information needed to provide features such as sessions, preferences, and user-requested memory features.

Your Choices

You may request changes or deletion of information associated with your account, subject to technical and legal requirements.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For questions about privacy, contact the Nova team.
