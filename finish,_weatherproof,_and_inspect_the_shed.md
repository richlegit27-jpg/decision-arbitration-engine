# Finish, Weatherproof, and Inspect the Shed

Before considering the shed complete, conduct a thorough inspection focusing on structure alignment, door operation, water resistance, and overall safety.

## 1. Inspect Structure Alignment

- Check that all walls are plumb and square.
- Verify roof trusses or rafters are correctly positioned and securely fastened.
- Ensure corners meet cleanly without gaps or misalignment.
- Confirm that the foundation or base is level and stable.

## 2. Test Door Operation

- Open and close all doors multiple times to ensure smooth operation.
- Adjust hinges and latches as needed for proper fit and security.
- Confirm that doors seal tightly when closed to prevent drafts and moisture entry.
- Ensure that locks or fasteners function correctly for security.

## 3. Check Water Resistance

- Inspect exterior siding, trim, and roof for gaps, cracks, or damage.
- Confirm that all seams are properly sealed with caulking or flashing.
- Verify that roofing materials overlap correctly and are securely attached.
- Test water runoff by gently spraying water on the roof and walls, checking for leaks inside.
- Make sure gutters and downspouts (if installed) effectively direct water away from the shed.

## 4. Assess Overall Safety

- Confirm that there are no exposed nails, sharp edges, or splinters.
- Check that electrical installations (if any) are safe and up to code.
- Verify that ventilation is adequate to prevent moisture buildup.
- Ensure the shed is securely anchored to its foundation to withstand wind.
- Review surrounding area to ensure clear and safe access to and around the shed.

## Completion

Once all inspections are complete and any issues are addressed, the shed can be considered finished, weatherproofed, and safe for use.
