# Specification Document: Criteria for an Earth-like World

## Purpose
To define explicit, measurable minimum and ideal criteria that characterize a world as Earth-like for this project’s target selection and assessment.

---

## 1. Gravity

| Criterion       | Minimum         | Ideal           | Unit      | Notes                          |
|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------|--------------------------------|
| Surface gravity | 0.8 – 1.2       | ~1              | g (Earth=1) | Supports human physiology, structural integrity |
| Gravity range   | 7.8 – 11.8      | 9.8             | m/s²       | Equates to above 0.8–1.2 g      |

---

## 2. Atmospheric Composition

| Gas               | Minimum (%)                   | Ideal (%)                    | Notes                       |
|-------------------|------------------------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Nitrogen (N₂)     | ≥ 70                         | 78                          | Main inert component         |
| Oxygen (O₂)        | 19 – 23                      | 21                          | Supports aerobic life        |
| Carbon dioxide (CO₂) | ≤ 0.1                      | ~0.04                       | Avoids greenhouse overheat    |
| Water vapor (H₂O)  | Variable, trace amounts      | ~0.5 – 4                    | Supports hydrological cycle   |
| Trace gases & others | Similar in ratio to Earth   | Matches Earth                | For biosphere and climate     |

---

## 3. Atmospheric Pressure

| Criterion         | Minimum         | Ideal           | Units      | Notes                                      |
|-------------------|-----------------|-----------------|------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Surface pressure  | ≥ 0.5 bar       | 0.9 – 1.1 bar   | bar        | Maintains liquid water and human breathing |
| Pressure stability| Moderate fluctuations ±10%  | ±5% or less      | bar        | Avoids extreme climate instability          |

---

## 4. Magnetic Shielding

| Criterion         | Minimum                    | Ideal                     | Notes                                           |
|-------------------|----------------------------|---------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Magnetic field    | Detectable, > 0.1 μT       | Earth-like ~25–65 μT      | Protects atmosphere from solar wind stripping   |
| Field stability   | Stable enough to maintain atmosphere | Continuous with minimal reversals | Ensures long-term atmospheric retention           |

---

## 5. Liquid Water Coverage

| Criterion             | Minimum                       | Ideal                       | Units          | Notes                                 |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------------------|----------------|-------------------------------------|
| Surface liquid water  | ≥ 1% of surface area           | ≥ 50% of surface area       | % surface area | Supports hydrological cycle & biosphere |
| Water salinity        | Tolerable range for life       | Near Earth ocean salinity   | ppt (parts per thousand) | Supports diverse aquatic ecosystems      |

---

## 6. Temperature Stability (Surface)

| Criterion             | Minimum range                 | Ideal range                   | Units        | Notes                                           |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|--------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Mean surface temp     | -10°C to 40°C                 | 0°C to 30°C                   | °C           | Supports liquid water, broad habitability        |
| Diurnal temperature variation | ≤ 30°C (max-min)          | ≤ 15°C                        | °C           | Avoids extreme thermal stress on life forms      |
| Seasonal variation    | Present but moderate           | Similar to Earth’s seasons    | °C           | Allows ecological cycles without disruption      |

---

## 7. Biosphere Complexity

| Criterion                  | Minimum                                 | Ideal                                  | Notes                                         |
|----------------------------|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Presence of microbial life  | Confirmed or high likelihood             | Established diverse microbial ecosystems | Essential for soil and nutrient cycling         |
| Plant analogs              | Simple autotrophs or microbial mats      | Diverse flora analogous to Earth’s plants | Supports food webs and oxygen production        |
| Animal analogs              | Present if possible; microbial-based food web minimum | Multicellular fauna with complex ecosystems | Indicates advanced biosphere development          |

---

## 8. Human Habitability Thresholds

| Criterion                   | Minimum                                     | Ideal                                      | Notes                                        |
|-----------------------------|---------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Radiation levels            | ≤ 2 times Earth background radiation         | Earth-equivalent or better                  | Ensures human safety without shielding complications |
| Toxin levels in atmosphere | Negligible or absent for known toxins (CO, H₂S) | Earth-like clean air                         | Supports direct human respiration              |
| Food & water availability  | Sustainable natural sources or possibilities for terraforming | Readily accessible natural resources          | Critical for long-term colonization             |
| Temperature & pressure    | Within human tolerance limits (see above)       | Earth-normal conditions                      | Avoids need for extreme habitats                |

---

## Summary

| Parameter            | Minimum to qualify Earth-like          | Ideal Earth-like                   |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Gravity              | 0.8 – 1.2 g                          | 1 g                              |
| Atmosphere           | N₂ ≥70%, O₂ 19–23%, CO₂ ≤0.1%, sea-level pressure ≥ 0.5 bar | Earth-like composition & pressure |
| Magnetic Shielding   | Detectable magnetic field >0.1 μT     | Earth-like stable field ~50 μT    |
| Water Coverage       | ≥1% surface liquid water               | ≥50% surface liquid water           |
| Temperature Range    | Daily average -10 to 40°C              | 0 to 30°C average with mild variations  |
| Biosphere            | Confirmed microbial life                | Complex multi-tier biosphere       |
| Habitability         | Radiation ≤ 2x Earth, negligible toxins, human-tolerable T & P | Earth-equivalent safety and comfort  |
