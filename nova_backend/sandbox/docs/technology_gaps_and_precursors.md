Technology Gap Register with Linked Precursor Programs

1. Large-Scale Closed Ecological Systems (CCES)
   - Gap Description: Current closed ecological systems are not scalable or reliable enough for long-duration habitat support on a planetary scale.
   - Impact: Limits ability to maintain life-support without resupply.
   - Precursor Programs / Experiments:
     - Biosphere 2 Extended Duration Studies – Conduct new cycles focusing on system stability and failure modes.
     - MELiSSA (Micro-Ecological Life Support System Alternative) Advancement – Scale up closed-loop systems with real-time control.
     - ISS Plant Habitat Experiments – Long-duration plant growth with full recycling of air and water.
     - Controlled Ecological Life Support System (CELSS) Testbeds – Integrate microbial and plant systems for waste recycling and food production in terrestrial labs.

2. Radiation Shielding at Habitat Scale
   - Gap Description: Lack of validated, scalable radiation shielding solutions for habitats exposed to cosmic and solar radiation over extended periods.
   - Impact: Limits crew safety and mission duration.
   - Precursor Programs / Experiments:
     - NASA's RadShield and analogous shielding material development – test candidate materials in LEO and Lunar orbit.
     - Lunar Regolith Shielding Demonstrations – Small-scale habitat test modules buried with regolith.
     - Materials Testing on Deep Space Gateway platform – aggregate data on radiation attenuation.
     - Biological Countermeasures Research – Study human radiation tolerance increases.

3. Atmospheric Processing and Regulation
   - Gap Description: Immature technologies for maintaining stable breathable atmospheres within habitats with variable metabolic loads.
   - Impact: Risk of atmosphere degradation or buildup of toxic components.
   - Precursor Programs / Experiments:
     - ISS Atmosphere Control and Monitoring upgrades – advanced sensor arrays and feedback loops.
     - Mars and Lunar habitats analog projects – demonstration of atmospheric scrubbers and oxygen regeneration.
     - Prototype in-situ atmosphere recycling units – test with real human metabolic loads in analog environments.
     - Testing catalytic converters and bioreactors for gas balance.

4. In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)
   - Gap Description: Technologies for extracting and processing water, oxygen, metals, and building materials from local planetary bodies.
   - Impact: Critical for reducing Earth dependency and enabling large scale infrastructure.
   - Precursor Programs / Experiments:
     - MOXIE (Mars Oxygen In-Situ Experiment) – expand scale and duration.
     - Regolith extraction and processing pilot plants – Earth-based lunar or Martian soil simulant tests.
     - Water ice mining prototypes – test systems for excavation, purification, and storage.
     - Additive manufacturing with local materials – demonstration on ISS or Lunar Gateway.

5. Autonomous Construction Systems
   - Gap Description: Robust autonomous robotics capable of constructing habitats and infrastructure on planetary surfaces without Earth intervention.
   - Impact: Enables sustainable infrastructure buildup with minimal human presence.
   - Precursor Programs / Experiments:
     - NASA RASSOR and similar regolith moving robots – extended field tests in analog environments.
     - Additive manufacturing robots for habitat construction – Earth and space tests.
     - Autonomous assembly demonstrations on Lunar Gateway or robotic landers.
     - AI-driven construction planning and fault management – simulations and analog tests.

6. Long-Duration Biosphere Management
   - Gap Description: Methods for sustaining complex biospheres with multi-generational ecosystems over years or decades.
   - Impact: Essential for permanent human presence and ecosystem stability.
   - Precursor Programs / Experiments:
     - Long-term ecological research (LTER) stations adapted to closed systems.
     - Controlled biosphere ecosystem experiments – monitor species interactions, nutrient cycling.
     - Advanced life support integration trials in analog habitats.
     - Automated environmental monitoring and adaptive management systems.
