# Build and Level the Foundation

## Overview

This guide covers the installation of the chosen foundation system for your shed project, ensuring the foundation is level, square, and stable to support the structure properly.

---

## Materials and Tools Needed

- Chosen foundation materials (concrete blocks, poured concrete, gravel, piers, or skid foundation materials)
- Level (preferably a laser level or spirit level)
- Measuring tape
- Carpenter’s square
- Shovel
- Tamper or compactor
- String and stakes
- Gravel or sand (if needed for leveling)
- Concrete mix and tools (if using poured concrete)
- Protective gloves and safety gear

---

## Step 1: Prepare the Site

1. Clear the building area of any vegetation, rocks, or debris.
2. Mark your foundation layout using stakes and string according to your shed dimensions.
3. Verify that the corners are square by measuring diagonals; adjust strings until both diagonals are equal.

---

## Step 2: Excavate and Level Subgrade

1. Dig down to the required foundation depth according to your foundation type and local code.
2. Remove loose soil and compact the subgrade using a tamper.
3. Add a layer of gravel or sand and level it to create a stable base for the foundation materials.

---

## Step 3: Install Foundation System

### Concrete Blocks

1. Place blocks on the prepared base in the layout pattern.
2. Use a level to ensure each block is level and in line with adjacent blocks.
3. Adjust by adding or removing base material as needed.
4. Continuously check that the foundation remains level and square as you proceed.

### Poured Concrete

1. Build formwork according to layout dimensions.
2. Pour concrete evenly within forms.
3. Use a screed (a long, straight board or metal screed tool) to level the surface and remove excess concrete.
4. Check level and square regularly during curing using a level and carpenter's square.

### Pier Footings or Skid Foundation

1. Dig holes at marked locations according to your design.
2. Fill holes with concrete or place pre-cast piers ensuring they are securely set.
3. Use a level to confirm piers are flat and at uniform height.
4. Lay skids (beams) on the leveled piers or directly on the prepared ground, ensuring they are straight and level.

---

## Step 4: Verify Level and Square

1. Use a level (laser or spirit) to ensure all foundation surfaces are flat and plumb.
2. Double-check corner alignment with a carpenter's square.
3. Measure diagonals again to confirm the foundation is square.
4. Make any necessary adjustments immediately before the materials set or stabilize.

---

## Step 5: Allow Foundation to Settle

1. If poured concrete is used, allow adequate curing time as recommended by the concrete manufacturer:
   - Typically 24-48 hours before light work begins,
   - 7 days for partial strength,
   - 28 days for full strength.
2. For block or pier foundations, ensure stability without any shifting before proceeding with shed construction.

---

## Final Notes

- A properly installed, level, and square foundation prevents structural issues and extends the life of your shed.
- Confirm local building codes and permits before starting your foundation work.
- If unsure about foundation requirements or installation, consult a professional for guidance.
