# Project Specification

## Project Title
Make a Lighter

## Objective
To design and build a portable, reliable, and easy-to-use lighter that provides a consistent flame for lighting cigarettes, candles, and other items.

## Scope
- Develop a functional prototype of a lighter
- Ensure safety mechanisms are integrated
- Design for portability and ergonomic use
- Utilize commonly available materials for ease of manufacturing and maintenance
- Provide lasting fuel capacity and simple refueling options

## Features
- Flame ignition system (e.g., piezoelectric or flint spark)
- Adjustable flame height
- Safety lock to prevent accidental ignition
- Durable casing resistant to minor impacts
- Wind-resistant flame design

## Deliverables
- Fully assembled lighter prototype
- User manual with operating instructions and safety guidelines
- Technical drawings and specifications
- Testing report covering safety and performance

## Timeline
- Research and design: 2 weeks
- Prototype assembly: 3 weeks
- Testing and refinement: 2 weeks
- Documentation and final review: 1 week

## Success Criteria
- Reliable ignition and flame maintenance
- Compliance with safety standards
- Positive preliminary user feedback on usability and design
- Scalability for small-scale manufacturing
